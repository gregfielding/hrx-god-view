How it works

Sign in with your mobile number

We text you a 6-digit code. There is no password to remember.

Apply to a shift or role

Tap a posting, review the requirements, and apply. A recruiter reaches out by text or phone.

Confirm and go to work

Confirm when the app asks, follow the check-in instructions, and your hours flow to payroll.
