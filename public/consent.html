SMS and Mobile Communications Consent Agreement

Effective Date: October 21, 2025 · Last Updated: October 21, 2025

Applies to: C1 Staffing, LLC and its affiliates, including HRX One, HRX Companion, and related products ("we," "us," "our").

Summary: By creating an account and selecting "I agree" during sign up, you consent to receive text messages (SMS/MMS), emails, and mobile app notifications about job opportunities, scheduling, onboarding, payroll, and related employment communications. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS. You can manage push notifications in your device settings.

1. Purpose of This Consent

This Consent authorizes us to send you communications via SMS/MMS text messages, email, and mobile app push notifications relating to your application, job opportunities, onboarding, scheduling, timekeeping, payroll, benefits, HR updates, security alerts (including multi-factor codes), and other communications relevant to your engagement with us.

Consent to receive these messages is not a condition of applying for or obtaining employment. Where required, we may obtain separate consent for any marketing communications unrelated to your employment or job search.

2. Types of Messages

You may receive, including but not limited to:

  • Job openings, shift offers, assignment confirmations, and schedule changes
  • Onboarding tasks, document reminders, I-9/E-Verify and compliance notices
  • Timecard reminders, payroll notices, and benefits updates
  • Account security alerts, verification codes, and app/service updates
  • Operational announcements required for workplace safety or policy changes

We will not send unrelated promotional/marketing messages without a separate, explicit opt-in.

3. Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your activity, location, and assignments, and may include multiple messages per week during active periods (e.g., onboarding or scheduled shifts).

4. Carrier & Cost Notice

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan and carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Availability may vary by carrier and device.

5. Opt-Out & Preference Management

  • SMS: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message from us. To get help, reply HELP.
  • Push Notifications: Manage or disable notifications in your device settings or in the app notification preferences.
  • Email: You may adjust email preferences using links in the footer where available. We may still send transactional or legally required emails.

Opting out of SMS or push notifications will not stop communications we are legally permitted or required to send by email or within your account.

6. Help & Support

For assistance with messaging, contact us at support@c1staffing.com.

7. Authorization & Representations

By creating an account and selecting "I agree" on the sign-up screen, you confirm that (i) you are the owner or authorized user of the phone number and email you provide; (ii) you authorize us to contact you as described in this Consent; and (iii) you understand you may revoke consent at any time using the methods above.

If your phone number or email changes, you agree to update your account information promptly to help ensure accurate delivery of communications.

8. Privacy

We handle your personal information in accordance with our SMS/Text Messaging & Mobile Communications Privacy Notice and Privacy Policy. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or your text messaging opt-in and consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

9. Changes to This Consent

We may update this Consent from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above will reflect the latest version. Material changes will be posted in the app or website. Continued use of our services after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the updated Consent.

10. Record of Consent

We maintain a record of your consent (including date/time, device, IP, and user account) for compliance purposes. You may request a copy of your consent record by contacting support@c1staffing.com.

11. Jurisdiction & Compliance Notices

This Consent is intended to comply with applicable laws and carrier policies, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and relevant state privacy laws. If any provision is found unenforceable in a particular jurisdiction, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remainder will continue in effect.

How opt-in works (for carriers and reviewers)

Workers opt in when they create an account at https://hrxone.com: they enter and verify their mobile phone number, then check the box "I agree" to this SMS and Mobile Communications Consent Agreement, which is linked on the sign-up screen. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help; consent is not a condition of employment. Workers can also turn text messages on or off at any time from their account dashboard.
